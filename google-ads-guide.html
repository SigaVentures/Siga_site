Understanding Google Ad Campaign Types

🎯

Google Search Ads

Text ads displayed at the top of Google search results when users search for high-intent keywords like "best digital marketing agency near me".

⚡

Performance Max (PMax)

AI-driven automated campaigns that run across YouTube, Display, Search, Discover, Gmail, and Google Maps simultaneously.

🛍️

Google Shopping Ads

Product listing ads featuring images, prices, and merchant names — ideal for e-commerce stores looking to drive direct transactions.

📺

YouTube & Display Ads

Visual and video ad formats designed to build brand awareness, retarget past site visitors, and generate demand across millions of partner sites.

5 Costly Google Ads Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Broad Match Without Negative Keywords: Allowing Google to show your ads for irrelevant search terms that waste budget.
  2. Sending Traffic to Homepage: Always send ad clicks to specialized, high-converting landing pages tailored to the ad copy.
  3. Ignoring Conversion Tracking: Failing to set up accurate conversion tracking means you cannot optimize for profit or ROAS.
  4. Set-and-Forget Mindset: Successful PPC requires active A/B testing of headlines, bidding strategies, and search query audits.
  5. Low Quality Score: Weak landing page experience and low ad relevance increase your cost-per-click (CPC).
