Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking a strategy call. Can't find your answer here? Reach out and we'll respond within one business day.

What services does SIGA Ventures offer?+

We offer SEO, performance marketing (Google Ads & Meta Ads), branding, web design and development, content marketing, social media marketing, video production and AI automation — either as standalone services or a combined growth package.

Which locations do you serve?+

We're based in Hosur and Chennai and actively serve clients across Tamil Nadu and Pondicherry, along with remote clients across India.

How long before I see results?+

SEO and content typically show meaningful movement in 60–90 days. Paid media (Google Ads, Meta Ads) can show performance signals within the first 2–4 weeks as campaigns are optimized.

Do you offer month-to-month contracts?+

Yes. Most engagements start with a 90-day roadmap, after which we move to a flexible month-to-month arrangement based on performance.

How do I get started?+

Book a free strategy call through our contact page. We'll review your current marketing, identify quick wins, and outline a plan before any commitment.

Do you work with businesses outside your listed industries?+

Yes — our team has delivered work across furniture, real estate, packaging, EV/automotive and more. If you're unsure whether we're a fit, ask us on the strategy call.
